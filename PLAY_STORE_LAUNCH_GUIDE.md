## 3. How to Pass the 12-Tester / 14-Day Google Play Requirement

### Step 1: Create a Google Group for One-Click Opt-In
1. Go to [Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/) and create a public group: `velvetrain-testers@googlegroups.com`.
2. Set permissions: Anyone on the web can join.
3. In Google Play Console under **Test and release > Closed testing**, create a track named "Closed Beta" and add `velvetrain-testers@googlegroups.com` as your tester email list.
4. Copy the two invite links generated by Google Play:
   * **Web Join Link:** `https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.velvetrain.app`
   * **Android Play Store Link:** (Provided by Play Console)

### Step 2: Recruit Testers on Reddit (r/AndroidClosedTesting)
Post on [r/AndroidClosedTesting](https://www.reddit.com/r/AndroidClosedTesting/) with this proven template:

> **Title:** [Test for Test] Need 15 testers for VelvetRain (Ambient Sleep & Focus Mixer). Will test back instantly!
> 
> **Body:**  
> Hey devs! I just published a closed beta for **VelvetRain**, an aesthetic offline rain and sleep sound mixer.  
> 
> I need 15 testers to keep it installed for 14 days. I will test your app back immediately, leave feedback, and keep it installed for the full 14 days on my dedicated test phone!  
> 
> 1. Join Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/velvetrain-testers  
> 2. Web opt-in link: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.velvetrain.app  
> 3. Play Store link: [Insert your Play Store test link]  
> 
> Drop your app links and a screenshot below, and I will install and review yours right away! Let's help each other launch! 🚀

### Step 3: During the 14 Days
* Target **20 to 25 testers** (to ensure you never drop below the 12-tester minimum if a couple uninstall).
* Open the app occasionally on your own test phone.
* On Day 5 and Day 10, push **1 minor update** (e.g. Version 1.0.1: "Improved audio fade-out smoothness"). This shows Google that your release pipeline is active.

### Step 4: Applying for Production Access
After 14 consecutive days with 12+ active testers, the "Apply for Production" button will unlock in the Play Console. Google asks 3 simple questions:
1. **How did you recruit testers?**  
   *Answer:* "Recruited organic early-adopter testers and peer developers through developer communities and sleep sound interest groups."
2. **What feedback did you collect?**  
   *Answer:* "Testers praised the OLED dark mode and gapless audio. Based on early feedback, we fine-tuned the audio balance between thunder and rain and optimized the sleep fade-out curve."
3. **What changes did you make?**  
   *Answer:* "Pushed version 1.0.1 to refine volume slider responsiveness and enhance sleep timer transition curves."

Google approves production access within 2 to 4 days after this!

## 4. Building the APK / AAB via GitHub Actions

You don't need Android Studio or Gradle installed on your Windows PC:
1. Create a GitHub repository (e.g. `velvet-rain`).
2. Push this folder to GitHub.
3. Click the **Actions** tab in your GitHub repository.
4. The workflow `.github/workflows/build-apk.yml` will automatically run on an Ubuntu cloud runner, compile the project, and upload `VelvetRain-Android-APK` under Artifacts.
5. Download the `.apk` directly to test on your phone!
