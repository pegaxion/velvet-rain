Privacy Policy for VelvetRain

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Summary (The Plain English Guarantee)

VelvetRain is built privacy-first. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal data, audio recordings, browsing history, or device identifiers. VelvetRain works 100% offline on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

VelvetRain is an offline ambient sound mixer application. We do not require you to create an account, enter an email address, or provide any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment credentials.
  • No Location Data: VelvetRain does not access, request, or track your GPS or network location.
  • No Microphone or Audio Recording: VelvetRain outputs ambient soundscapes procedurally; it never accesses or listens through your device microphone.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Trackers: We do not integrate advertising trackers, marketing SDKs, or invasive data brokers.

2. On-Device Local Storage

VelvetRain saves your user preferences (such as your customized sound volume levels, custom soundscape names, and OLED theme settings) locally on your device using standard mobile local storage (LocalStorage / Web Storage).

This information never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any cloud server. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

VelvetRain does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, VelvetRain is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards.

4. Permissions Used by VelvetRain

To deliver a seamless ambient sleep experience, VelvetRain uses only necessary device capabilities:

  • Foreground Audio & Background Media Playback: Allows soothing rain and ambient audio to continue playing when your screen is locked or while using other apps.
  • Wake Lock (Optional): Allows the display to remain gently dimmed if the user chooses not to lock the screen during meditation or timer countdowns.

5. Security of Your Information

Because all sound generation and presets are executed locally on your device, your data is as secure as your personal phone or tablet. We do not maintain any servers holding user logs.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Health, Wellness & Hearing Safety Disclaimer

Non-Medical Wellness Notice: VelvetRain is designed exclusively as an ambient sound relaxation, meditation, and focus tool for personal lifestyle and wellness. VelvetRain is NOT a medical device, nor does it provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, treatment, or cure for insomnia, sleep disorders, tinnitus, ADHD, anxiety, or any other medical or psychological condition. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare professional regarding any clinical concerns.

Hearing Safety & Responsible Usage: To protect your hearing, please listen at comfortable, moderate volume levels. Do not listen at excessive volumes through headphones for prolonged periods. Never use VelvetRain, binaural beats, or noise generators while operating a motor vehicle, riding a bicycle, operating heavy machinery, or engaging in any activity where ambient auditory awareness is required for safety.
